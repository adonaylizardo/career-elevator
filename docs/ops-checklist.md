# Career Elevator — Ops Checklist

Internal workflow for delivering an Opportunity Sheet after purchase.

## Flow

1. **Paid** — Customer completes payment via PayPal or crypto on `/checkout`.
2. **Intake complete** — Customer submits the Oracle FULL intake form on `/intake` with all required fields.
3. **Ally drafts sheet** — Ally builds the Opportunity Sheet: 10–20 matched roles, gap notes, market notes.
4. **Adonay QA** — Review Ally's draft for fit, accuracy, and tone before sending.
5. **Send** — Deliver the Opportunity Sheet to the customer.

## Before sending

- [ ] Payment confirmed
- [ ] Intake is complete (no missing required fields)
- [ ] Roles are open and links work
- [ ] Gap notes reference the customer's actual CV
- [ ] Market notes reflect current hiring patterns for their target
- [ ] Ally has completed the Opportunity Sheet draft
- [ ] Adonay has signed off on the draft

## Notes

- Delivery window: 3–5 days from a complete, aligned intake.
- If intake is unclear, clarify with the customer before starting the clock.
